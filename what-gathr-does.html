Gathr for organizers

The reunion that actually comes together.

Most group events don't fail because nobody wants to come. They fall apart because one person is managing an evite, a reply-all thread, a Google Form, and a spreadsheet — at midnight, alone. Gathr is the system that handles that work for you.

The stack you're replacing

An evite nobody updates

Nobody checks it after they RSVP. The guest list is wrong. You can't tell who's actually coming.

A reply-all thread

Started well-intentioned. Now it's 94 emails and three people keep responding to everyone.

A Google Form

Responses live in a spreadsheet you have to keep checking. Three tabs. Manual tallying.

Your own memory

Because someone has to remember who hasn't responded, who needs a reminder, and when to send the recap.

Gathr replaces that whole stack — not just with better tools, but with a system that reads the situation and tells you what to do next.

How it works

From "I should plan this" to "it's handled."

You set the event up. Gathr figures out what needs to happen next and puts the right action in front of you.

1

Create your event and build your list

Add the basics — date, location, what it is. Paste in your email list or drop a file. Gathr adds everyone to your roster and gets ready to send.

2

Invitations go out — with a link that actually works

Each person gets a personalized email with a one-click login. No password, no account creation. They click, they're in, they can RSVP in 10 seconds.

3

Gathr watches the RSVPs and tells you when to act

When responses are coming in, you'll know. When things slow down and a nudge is overdue, Gathr surfaces that too — with a suggested reminder ready to send.

4

Day before, the reminder goes out automatically

Confirmed attendees get "see you tomorrow." Anyone still undecided gets a last-chance ask. You don't have to remember to send either one.

5

After it ends, send the recap in two minutes

Write what you want to say. Gathr sends it to everyone who came, keeps a record, and closes the loop — so the event doesn't just disappear.

A full reference for organizers in the beta.

Getting people in

✓

Email import

Paste or drop a list of email addresses. Gathr builds your roster and queues up invitations.

✓

Share link

A link you can post anywhere — a Facebook group, a text thread, an email. Anyone who clicks can request to join. People already on your roster get fast-tracked straight to their dashboard.

✓

Approval flow

New join requests come to you for approval. One click to let someone in; they get a welcome email with their login link the moment you approve.

✓

One-click login for members

No passwords, no accounts to create. Members click a link in their email and they're in. Works on every device, adds to home screen on iOS and Android.

Emails Gathr sends for you

✓

Invitation email

Personalized, with a one-click login link. Includes a prompt to add Gathr to their home screen so they don't lose the link.

✓

Publish notification

When your event goes from teaser to live, all active members get notified that RSVPs are open.

✓

Manual reminder

Send from the dashboard whenever you want. Confirmed attendees get "see you soon." Anyone still undecided gets "RSVPs are still open." People who declined don't get either.

✓

Day-before reminder

Sends automatically the day before your event. Two variants based on each person's RSVP status. You don't have to do anything.

✓

Post-event recap

Write what you want to say, Gathr sends it to everyone. Closes the loop and gives people something to hold onto after it ends.

Tracking and management

✓

RSVP dashboard

See who's confirmed, who's declined, and who hasn't responded — always up to date, no spreadsheet required.

✓

Declined list

Know who said no and why — visible from the event card so you're never surprised by headcount.

✓

Session management

Break your event into multiple sessions — dinner Friday, activities Saturday. Members RSVP to each one separately.

✓

Notification bell

In-app alerts when something needs your attention. Dismissible. Links straight to the action.
